harness · a self-improving agent that has to earn its edits

Ask the database

Ask a question in plain language.

The agent writes one MongoDB query, runs it against the live sample_mflix database, and its rows are compared with a reference answer computed straight from the data. When the two disagree, that disagreement is what the gate acts on.

Waiting for a human

The gate's last refusal.

The gate decides on its own and it refused this. A person can still overrule it, and the overrule is written to MongoDB with who decided and why.
